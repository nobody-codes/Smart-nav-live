FINAL YEAR PROJECT

Real-Time Crowdsourced Mapping & Intelligent Navigation

A modern distributed web engineering platform combining crowdsourced telemetry, OpenStreetMap routing, bidirectional WebSockets, and dynamic road hazard avoidance algorithms.

1. Driver / User Role

Registered community drivers who navigate routes and crowdsource ground-truth road observations.

  • Report hazards (Accidents, Roadblocks, Traffic Jams, Floods, etc.)
  • Upload scene photos and geotagged coordinates
  • Plan intelligent shortest routes avoiding roadblocks
  • Earn contribution points and unlock tiered badges

2. Administrator Role

Municipal traffic moderators ensuring system integrity and real-time hazard verification.

  • Review pending crowdsourced reports
  • Approve valid hazards (triggers instant live broadcast & +10 pts)
  • Reject or purge fraudulent reports
  • Monitor Chart.js telemetry dashboards & export data

3. Guest (View-Only)

Casual travelers seeking quick situational awareness without requiring registration.

  • View live OpenStreetMap with clustered incident pins
  • Filter incidents by category and address
  • Inspect public traffic alerts in real time
  • Read-only access ensuring privacy and safety
ALGORITHM DEEP DIVE

How `NavigationService` Computes Intelligent Routes

Conventional routing engines often route motorists straight into road closures until severe delays occur. SmartNav employs a multi-stage intelligent routing pipeline:

Obstacle Detection Along Corridor

Calculates an elliptical corridor around the line connecting start and destination to identify any approved roadblock, flood, construction, or accident within the road path.

Dynamic Bypass Waypoint Synthesis

Generates perpendicular bypass detour coordinates around active roadblocks, allowing OpenStreetMap routing to navigate safely around impassable sections.

Congestion Weight Penalties & Red-Line Highlighting

Applies a configurable congestion penalty factor (1.8x) to estimated duration and highlights high-traffic segments with a bright red polyline on the driver's screen.
